========================================================================================
             SAMPLE CLINICAL ONCOLOGY PRACTICE GUIDELINE: NON-SMALL CELL LUNG CANCER
========================================================================================

SECTION 1: MOLECULAR TESTING AND BIOMARKER PROFILING
In patients with advanced or metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), comprehensive
molecular biomarker testing should be performed prior to initiating first-line systemic therapy.
Key targetable oncogenic drivers include EGFR mutations (exon 19 deletions, L858R substitution),
ALK rearrangements, ROS1 fusions, BRAF V600E mutations, RET fusions, MET exon 14 skipping
mutations, and KRAS G12C mutations. Additionally, PD-L1 expression levels should be measured
using immunohistochemistry (IHC) to guide immunotherapy selection.

SECTION 2: FIRST-LINE TARGETED THERAPY FOR EGFR-MUTANT NSCLC
For patients with EGFR exon 19 deletion or L858R mutation, third-generation EGFR tyrosine kinase
inhibitor (TKI) Osimertinib (AZD9291) is recommended as first-line monotherapy. In the FLAURA
trial, Osimertinib demonstrated statistically significant improvements in progression-free survival
(PFS) and overall survival (OS) compared to first-generation TKIs (Gefitinib or Erlotinib), with
a lower incidence of Grade 3 or higher adverse events.

SECTION 3: RESISTANCE MECHANISMS AND SECOND-LINE INTERVENTIONS
Acquired resistance to first-line Osimertinib frequently involves EGFR C797S point mutations,
MET amplification, or HER2 amplification. Upon disease progression, repeat liquid biopsy or
tissue re-biopsy is recommended to identify actionable resistance pathways. In the absence of
a targetable resistance mutation, platinum-based doublet chemotherapy (Cisplatin or Carboplatin
combined with Pemetrexed) plus Bevacizumab is indicated.

SECTION 4: IMMUNOTHERAPY IN PD-L1 HIGH EXPRESSING TUMORS
For patients with non-oncogene addicted NSCLC exhibiting high PD-L1 expression (Tumor Proportion
Score TPS >= 50%), Pembrolizumab monotherapy is recommended as first-line therapy based on the
KEYNOTE-024 trial. For TPS 1-49%, combination chemo-immunotherapy (Pembrolizumab + Carboplatin +
Pemetrexed) is indicated.
